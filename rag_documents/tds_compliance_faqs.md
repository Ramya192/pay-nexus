# TDS Compliance FAQs — Income Tax Department

Source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/%20tds%20compliance-faq (Government of India, Income Tax Department)

Note: this page covers the transition from the Income Tax Act 1961 to the Income Tax Act 2025 for TDS purposes — relevant background for Section 192 (TDS on salary) even though PayNexus's users are mostly asking about the FY2025-26 old-Act rules.

## Section 192 & Salary TDS

Transition rule: "Tax is required to be deducted at the time of payment. Thus, TDS on salary shall be governed by different Acts, based on the date of payment."

For FY 2025-26 salary payments through March 2026, the old Act's Section 192 applies. From April 2026 onward, employers must use Section 392(1) of the new Act and reset the TDS computation from 1st April 2026 for the new tax year.

## Core Transition Principle

The triggering event determines applicability: "If the earlier event occurs on or before 31st March 2026, the Income tax Act 1961 will be applicable. However, if the earlier event occurs on or after 1st April 2026, the provisions of the Income Tax Act 2025 shall be applicable."

## TDS Rates & Deadlines

No changes — "The TDS rates and monetary thresholds for all categories of payments have been retained." Deposit deadlines remain unchanged; late deposits incur interest at 1.5% monthly.

## System Updates Required

Organizations must update ERP and payroll systems to reflect new section numbering, terminology, and reporting requirements introduced by the Income Tax Act 2025.
