# FAQs on New Tax vs Old Tax Regime — Income Tax Department

Source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/new-tax-vs-old-tax-regime-faqs (Government of India, Income Tax Department)

## Overview

The Finance Act 2023 amended Section 115BAC effective AY 2024-25, establishing the new tax regime as the default for individuals, HUF, AOP, and related entities. Taxpayers may opt out to use the old system. For non-business income cases, regime selection occurs annually via ITR before the due date. Those with business/professional income must file Form 10-IEA to opt out of the new regime.

## Key Q&A Pairs

**Q1: What is the difference between old and new tax regimes?**
A: The tax slabs and rates are different in old and new tax regimes. Various deductions and exemptions are allowed in the old system. The new approach offers lower rates of taxes but permits limited deductions.

**Q2: Which regime is better?**
A: The choice depends on individual circumstances. Taxpayers should do a comparative evaluation and analysis under both regimes using the Income Tax Portal's calculator.

**Q3: Must employees notify employers of their regime choice?**
A: Yes, employee notification is required. Absence of communication means the employee continues to be in the default tax regime. However, the intimation made to the employer would not amount to exercising the option for officially opting out — separate ITR action is needed before the filing deadline.

**Q4: Can salaried taxpayers claim HRA in the new regime?**
A: No. House Rent Allowance (HRA) is exempted under section 10(13A) only in the old regime for employees.

**Q5: Is Rs. 50,000 standard deduction available in new regime?**
A: Yes. Standard deduction of Rs. 50,000 or the amount of salary, whichever is lower, is available for both old and new tax regimes from AY 2024-25 forward.

**Q6: Can Chapter-VIA deductions be claimed in new regime?**
A: Generally no. In new tax regime, Chapter-VIA deductions cannot be claimed, except deduction u/s 80CCD(2)/80CCH/80JJAA. To access other deductions, select the old regime via the "opting out option" in your ITR form's Personal Information or Part-A General section.

**Q7: Can interest on borrowed capital for self-occupied property be deducted in new regime?**
A: No. Interest on borrowed capital for self-occupied property is not deductible from house property income under the new system. Choosing the old regime allows this deduction.

**Q8: Do senior citizens receive special tax advantages in new regime?**
A: The old regime provides basic exemption limits of Rs. 3,00,000 for senior citizens and Rs. 5,00,000 for super seniors. Under the new regime, no income tax is payable up to total income of Rs. 7 lakh.

**Q9: How does section 87A rebate differ between regimes?**
A: Old regime: rebate of 100 percent of income tax subject to a maximum of Rs. 12,500 for residents with income under Rs. 5,00,000. New regime (from 01-04-2024): rebate equals 100% of tax or Rs. 25,000 (whichever is less) for income under Rs. 7,00,000; partial rebate applies for higher incomes.

**Q10: Must Form 10-IEA be filed before ITR for opting into old regime?**
A: Only those who file ITR-3, ITR-4 or ITR-5 have to submit Form 10-IEA if they have business income. Individuals and HUFs filing their returns in Forms ITR-1 or 2 are not required to submit Form 10-IEA — they simply check the opt-out box.

**Q11: Can regime choices be switched annually?**
A: For business/professional income filers: once they opt out, they have only one chance for switching to new regime. Once they switch back to the new regime, they won't be able to choose old regime anytime in future. Non-business filers may switch yearly.

**Q12: If I switched regimes in prior years, am I automatically in old regime for AY 2024-25?**
A: No. New tax regime is default regime for AY 2024-25. Any actions in any previous years with respect to choice of regimes will not be applicable. You must submit Form 10-IEA again if opting for the old regime.

**Q13: Can Form 10-IEA be withdrawn within the same assessment year?**
A: No. Once Form 10IEA is filed for AY 2024-25, then it cannot be revoked / withdrawn in same AY. Withdrawal requires filing in the following year.

**Q14: Which form applies to ITR-5 filers seeking to opt out?**
A: Form 10-IEA is applicable to AOP's (other than Co-operative society) or BOI or AJP, who are filing return of Income in ITR-5 for AY 2024-25. Form 10-IFA applies to new manufacturing co-operative societies under Section 115BAE.
