# State-wise Professional Tax Slabs

**Compiled from public secondary sources (motilaloswal.com, cleartax.in, bankbazaar.com, and
others), cross-checked across multiple sources — NOT the verbatim state Acts.** Professional Tax is
a STATE-level tax (Article 276), so it's set independently by each state government and changes
without a Union Budget — treat these as approximate and verify against the specific state's current
notification before relying on them; only 4 of India's states/UTs that levy Professional Tax are
covered here, matching what PayNexus's users are most likely to ask about.

The ₹2,500/year cap under Article 276(2) applies to every state's Professional Tax, though most
states' slabs don't reach that maximum in practice.

## Maharashtra (monthly)

| Monthly salary | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹7,500 | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| ₹7,501 – ₹10,000 | ₹175/month | ₹0 |
| Above ₹10,000 | ₹200/month (₹300 in February) | ₹0 up to ₹25,000, then ₹200/month |

Maximum annual: ₹2,500 (male), ₹2,400 (female) — women below ₹25,000/month are exempt entirely.

## Karnataka (monthly)

Effective April 2025: salaries up to ₹25,000/month are exempt; above that, ₹200/month flat.
(This threshold has moved before and may move again — Karnataka has revised it more than once in
recent years, so this is the figure most likely to be stale by the time this is read.)

## Telangana (monthly)

| Monthly salary | Tax |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹15,000 | ₹0 |
| ₹15,001 – ₹20,000 | ₹150/month |
| Above ₹20,000 | ₹200/month |

## Tamil Nadu (half-yearly — NOT monthly, unlike the three states above)

Tamil Nadu deducts Professional Tax twice a year (August, covering April–September; January,
covering October–March), based on average half-yearly income, rather than monthly like most other
states. Greater Chennai Corporation slabs (other municipalities may differ slightly):

| Half-yearly income | Tax |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹21,000 | ₹0 |
| ₹21,000 – ₹30,000 | ₹180 |
| ₹30,000 – ₹45,000 | ₹425 |
| ₹45,000 – ₹60,000 | ₹930 |
| ₹60,000 – ₹75,000 | ₹1,025 |
| Above ₹75,000 | ₹1,250 |

At the top slab this works out to roughly ₹208/month averaged — one of the lower payroll compliance
costs among the states that levy Professional Tax at all.
